What does the challenge simulator do?

The simulator lets you recreate the conditions of a real challenge: account size, profit target, and daily and overall drawdown. Then you can see how your trades play out under those rules and where you might violate drawdown.

It is the best way to find out whether your strategy is compatible with the challenge rules — before you spend any money.

What can you practice?

With the simulator you can practice drawdown calculation (daily and overall), per-trade risk relative to the account, and the right position size (lot size) across symbols like EUR/USD, GBP/USD, Gold, US30, and US100.

You can also test your risk-to-reward ratio (R:R), take profit, and stop loss across different scenarios.

Why use the simulator before buying?

Most traders fail a challenge from violating drawdown, not from lack of profit. Practicing with the simulator helps you get a real feel for the risk limits before paying, and pick the plan that matches your capacity.
